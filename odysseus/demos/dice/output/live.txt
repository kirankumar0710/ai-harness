## Easy customizations
- **Name, address, and hours:** replace the placeholder text.
- **Colors:** edit the CSS variables at the top of `<style>`.
- **Images:** swap the Unsplash URLs for your own photos.
- **Contact form:** it currently shows an alert. Connect it to a service like Formspree or Netlify Forms to receive real messages.
